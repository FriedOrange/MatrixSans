Matrix Sans.

Introduction.

Succinct intro, free/open source, download link

Meet the family.

6 styles + mono

Matrix Sans Regular has a plain pixel look, like the bitmap fonts used in retro games.

Matrix Sans Video is interpolated to double the resolution, mimicking the on-screen displays of VCRs and camcorders and giving the feel of analogue video. The characters 2, 6, 9 and D have special patterns for extra authenticity.

Matrix Sans Screen has discrete square dots that make it resemble a character LCD or electronic sign..

Matrix Sans Print has discrete round dots giving it the look of a dot-matrix printer or flip-dot display.

Matrix Sans Raster has soft lines, emulating a monochrome CRT monitor from the days of 8-bit computers.

Matrix Sans Smooth is interpolated like the Video style, but taken to the extreme. It has perfectly straight diagonals in place of jagged steps, putting a modern twist on the genre.

Every style above is also available in the Matrix Mono family, a monospaced companion to Matrix Sans with a little more historical accuracy. You could even use it for programming!

Finally, every abovementioned font comes with an “SC” version where the small letters are replaced by small capitals. Use these wherever you want small caps but can’t use the OpenType smcp feature.
